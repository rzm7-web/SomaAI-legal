Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Nathan Deleau and Mateo Goury ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses your information when you use the Soma AI mobile application (the "App"). By downloading, accessing, or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. Introduction

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we may share it with, and the choices you have regarding your information. Please read this policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access or use the App.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account Information: such as your name, email address, date of birth, and password when you create an account.
  • Profile and Health-Related Information: such as height, weight, body measurements, fitness goals, training preferences, and other information you voluntarily provide to personalize your experience.
  • Photos and Media: such as images you choose to upload for body analysis, progress tracking, or profile purposes.
  • Usage Data: such as how you interact with the App, features used, session duration, and device performance data.
  • Device Information: such as device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and mobile network information.
  • Communications: such as messages, feedback, or support requests you send to us.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the App and its features;
  • Personalize your experience and generate tailored recommendations;
  • Process and analyze the content you submit, including photos, to deliver the core functionality of the App;
  • Communicate with you, including responding to inquiries and sending service-related notifications;
  • Monitor and improve the performance, security, and reliability of the App;
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues, fraud, or misuse;
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Health and Body Data

A core feature of the App involves analyzing photos you voluntarily upload of your body in order to generate body composition estimates, physique scoring, and personalized training and nutrition recommendations. This photo and body-related data may be considered sensitive personal information (including health-related information) under certain data protection laws, and we treat it with additional care.

By using this feature of the App, you understand and agree that:

  • Photos you upload for body analysis are processed using automated artificial intelligence image-analysis technology, provided by our third-party AI processing partner, in order to generate estimates such as body fat percentage, muscle development, posture, and proportions;
  • These estimates are generated automatically and are provided for informational and motivational purposes only; they are not medical diagnoses and should not be treated as clinical or medical advice;
  • Your photos and derived body-analysis data are stored securely and are never made public or shared with other users without your explicit, separate consent (for example, if you choose to opt in to an optional public leaderboard feature);
  • You may delete your uploaded photos and associated body-analysis data at any time by contacting us at somaaisupport@gmail.com or through the account deletion options available in the App;
  • Providing body photos is entirely optional; certain features of the App may not be available if you choose not to provide this information.

5. Data Storage

Your information is stored using secure third-party infrastructure providers. We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Data may be stored and processed in countries other than your country of residence, which may have data protection laws that differ from those in your jurisdiction.

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide the App's services, comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time, as described in the "User Rights" section below.

6. Third-Party Services

We rely on the following categories of third-party service providers to operate the App. These providers process your information solely on our behalf, under their own applicable privacy and security terms, and only to the extent necessary to provide their respective services:

  • Supabase — provides our backend infrastructure, including database hosting, user authentication, and secure file storage for account data, profile data, and uploaded photos.
  • OpenAI — provides the artificial intelligence models used to analyze body photos and food photos you submit, to generate body composition estimates, and to power certain conversational coaching features within the App.
  • Apple (App Store / StoreKit) — processes in-app purchases and subscriptions, as described in the "In-App Purchases" section below.
  • Push notification and analytics providers — may be used to deliver notifications and to help us understand aggregate App usage in order to improve performance and reliability.

We may add, remove, or change service providers over time as the App evolves. Any such providers are contractually required to implement appropriate safeguards and to use your information only for the purposes we authorize.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

7. Analytics

We may use analytics tools to understand how users interact with the App. These tools may collect information such as feature usage, session length, and general usage patterns. This information is used in aggregate form to improve the App and is not used to personally identify you beyond what is necessary to provide our services.

Payment information is processed directly by Apple in accordance with Apple's own privacy policy and terms; we do not have access to your full payment card details. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at somaaisupport@gmail.com. We will respond to your request within a reasonable timeframe and in accordance with applicable law.
