# Privacy Policy — Soma AI

**Last updated:** October 16, 2026

This policy explains what data the Soma AI mobile app (the "App") collects, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and how to exercise your rights. The App is offered worldwide.

## 1. Data controller

The data controller is Nathan Deleau and Mateo Goury, natural persons ("we", "us"). No company operates Soma AI.

**Contact:** somaaisupport@gmail.com

## 2. Data we collect

**Account**

- Account identifier and, depending on the sign-in method you choose: email address and password (stored hashed by our authentication provider), or the identifier provided by Sign in with Apple or Google, and the name shared by that service, if any.
- Before you create an account, an anonymous guest session is created to save your questionnaire answers. That session is a technical account with no email address until it is converted.

**Profile and questionnaire**

- First name, sex, age (a number you enter, not a date of birth), height, current and target weight, goal, training level, training days and session length, equipment and training location, daily activity level, eating habits, body type, language.

**Optional scan note**

- An optional note attached to a scan (a tag and up to 120 characters of text). It is stored with the scan on our servers. It is **not** included in the request sent to OpenAI for body analysis.

**Photos**

- Body photos you take or choose for a body scan (front, back and, optionally, sides).
- Meal photos you take or choose for a nutrition analysis.
- A target-physique image, if you provide one.

**Results and tracking**

- Scan results: estimated body fat percentage, muscle level, posture, proportions, strengths and areas to improve, Goal Match score (computed by our servers from those estimates).
- Training program, completed workouts, loads and personal records, logged meals and nutrition targets, active-day streak.
- Messages exchanged with the AI coach. The history is stored on our servers.

**Health data on the device — only if you allow it**

- On iPhone: step count and active calories, read from Apple Health, read-only.
- On Android: the same two measurements, read from Health Connect, read-only.
- They are used to show the day's activity on the device. They are **not** sent to our servers or to OpenAI, and they are never used for advertising. The App does not write any data to Apple Health or Health Connect.

**Purchases and referrals**

- Subscription status and purchase history (no card details), referral code and related rewards.
- The invited person's first name and referral status, visible to the person who issued the referral code (see [Terms of Use](https://rzm7-web.github.io/SomaAI-legal/terms-of-use.html)).

**Technical data**

- Push notification token, platform and App version, notification preferences.
- AI feature usage counters (account identifier, feature used, date) used to apply daily limits.
- History of your AI consent choices (choice, text version, date, language, platform, App version).
- Home-screen widgets (streak, calories, next workout, Goal Match): these displays stay on the device.

The App does not include advertising, ad tracking, audience analytics, or crash reporting. It has no public leaderboard.

## 3. Why we use this data

We use your data to run the App (account, program, nutrition, tracking), manage subscriptions and referrals, send notifications if you allow them, and keep the service secure. Artificial-intelligence features and Apple Health or Health Connect are used only if you consent. We do not sell your data and we do not use targeted advertising.

## 4. Artificial intelligence and sharing with OpenAI

Some features use OpenAI. The App asks for your agreement **before** sending personal data to OpenAI. Until you accept, those features are not started. Your choice is stored with your account.

| Feature | Data sent to OpenAI | Purpose |
|--------|---------------------|---------|
| Scan photo check | One photo, its angle, the language | Check that the photo can be used |
| Body scan | 2 to 5 body photos, the target-physique image if you provided one, sex, age, height, weight, target weight, level, training days, goal | Estimate body composition, muscle development, posture, and proportions |
| Meal analysis | One meal photo | Estimate the dish name, calories, and macronutrients |
| AI coach | Your message, the last 8 messages, first name, goal, level, equipment, session length and number of sessions, latest scan results, Goal Match trend, strengths, priorities, target-physique description, date of the last scan, current streak, today's intake, program summary | Answer your questions and suggest adjustments |
| Program generation | Questionnaire answers (including first name and body type when provided) and scan results | Build your personalized program |
| Program adaptation | Current and previous scan results, questionnaire answers, program summary | Adjust the program after a new scan |

A message to the coach may include information you type yourself, such as pain or an injury. That text is stored on our servers and, with the last eight messages, sent to OpenAI if you have accepted sharing.

AI results are estimates for information and motivation. They are not a diagnosis and they are not medical advice.

**If you decline:** the App remains usable. Your program and nutrition targets are calculated on the device from your answers, then stored on our servers. There is no scan, no body-fat estimate, and no Goal Match score. Body scan, meal analysis, and the AI coach stay unavailable.

**Change or withdraw your choice:** at any time in **Profile → Privacy → AI data sharing (OpenAI)**. Withdrawal applies to future sends. It does not delete results already stored in your account. If the consent text changes in a material way, we will ask for your agreement again.

Sends go through our servers. OpenAI processes data under its own [privacy policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy). Under OpenAI's standard API terms, customer content sent through the API is not used to train OpenAI's models unless separately enabled on the API account. OpenAI may retain certain content for a limited period for safety and abuse monitoring, as described in its documentation.

## 5. Processors and recipients

Your data is never sold. We share it only with providers needed to run the App:

- **Supabase** — accounts, database, and photo storage (hosted in the United States).
- **OpenAI** — AI analysis, only if you have accepted.
- **Apple** — Sign in with Apple, App Store purchases, push notifications, and Apple Health (read on the device).
- **Google** — Google sign-in, Health Connect, Google Play, and notifications, if you use them.
- **RevenueCat** — subscription status linked to your Soma AI account.
- **Expo** — push notification delivery.

Some of these providers are in the United States. We may also disclose data when the law requires it.

## 6. Retention and deletion

Your data on our servers is kept while your account or guest session exists, until you delete the account in the App (**Profile → Delete account**). That deletes photos, coach messages, and other account data on our servers. It does not cancel an active subscription and does not remove all purchase records at Apple, Google, or RevenueCat.

Steps, active calories, and widgets stay on the device. **No automatic inactivity deletion is turned on today.**

After account deletion, data is removed from our active systems. Backup copies at our providers may persist for a limited time according to their policies.

## 7. Sensitive data

Body photos, body-composition estimates, health information you write to the coach, and Apple Health or Health Connect data may be health data. Sharing with OpenAI and reading Apple Health or Health Connect rely on your consent. This data is never made public, is not shared with other users, and is not used for advertising. Steps and active calories do not leave the device.

## 8. Security

- Traffic between the App and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS).
- Access to data is limited per user: each account can read only its own data.
- Photos are stored in private spaces and are available only through temporary links.
- Keys for AI services stay on our servers.

No system is perfectly secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of your data, to receive a copy, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

In the App you can:

- **Export data** — Profile → Export data
- **Delete account** — Profile → Delete account
- **Withdraw AI consent** — Profile → Privacy → AI data sharing (OpenAI)

For any other request, contact us at **somaaisupport@gmail.com**. We respond within a reasonable time and as required by applicable law.

If you are not satisfied after contacting us, you may lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your country of residence. In France, this is the CNIL ([cnil.fr](https://www.cnil.fr)).

## 10. Minimum age

The App is not intended for anyone under 16. It does not check age at sign-up. If we learn that someone under 16 has sent us data, we will delete it.

## 11. Changes

We may update this policy. If a change is material, we will tell you in the App. If the change concerns sharing with the AI, we will ask for your consent again.
